# Rested XP in WoW Forever

Research compiled 19 November 2026.

**Summary:** Rested XP builds while your character rests, mostly while logged out. It is spent only on mob kills, which give double XP until the pool runs out. Where you log out decides how fast it builds.

## How you get it

- **Where:** Inns and capital cities are rested zones. In them you gain 5% of your current level every 8 hours, about 15% of a level per day.
- **Cap:** 150% of the XP needed for your current level, shown as 30 bubbles on the XP bar. Filling it from empty takes about 10 days.
- **Cozy Sleeping Bag:** Several guides say logging out inside the bag counts as resting, with an instant logout and full rested gain. This lets you log out properly far from any inn. The questline for it unlocks at level 14 and starts in Westfall or The Barrens.
- **Leatherworking Camp Tent:** It only tops a low pool up to about 5% of a level. It does nothing if you already have more than 5% banked, and it has a one-hour cooldown. It is only useful when your rested XP is nearly empty.
- **Well Rested legacy perk (Adventure tree, 5 ranks):** Each rank adds 4% to accrual speed and 4% to the cap. At 5/5 the cap is 180%. Skip it on your main, since early legacy points are better spent elsewhere, and take it on alts.
- **Per character:** Rested XP is not account-wide, so each alt builds its own pool.

## How logging out affects it

| Where you log out | Rested gain | Logout |
|---|---|---|
| Inn, capital city, or Cozy Sleeping Bag | Full rate (5% of a level per 8 hours) | Instant |
| Anywhere else | A quarter of the full rate | 20-second countdown |

- Before logging out, check that your portrait shows the "zzz".
- Being offline is not what matters, being rested is. Time spent online in a rested zone counts at the same rate.
- For a short break, logging out in the open costs little. For an overnight or longer break, the difference is large: about 15% of a level per day in an inn against about 4% outside.
- Logging out in an inn or capital right after a level-up is ideal. Each bubble is worth a percentage of your current level, so the same time offline banks more XP at a higher level.

## What to do once you have it

**What uses it up:** only kills, in the open world or in dungeons. Quest turn-ins, exploration and profession skill-ups do not use the pool. Turning in a quest moves the rested marker forward with your XP bar, so quest XP never wastes rested XP. Rested XP only pays off while you are killing mobs.

**How to spend it well:**

1. **Kill-heavy content:** Use it on grinding, kill quests and dungeons. In a group, kill XP is split and rested XP doubles your own share.
2. **Stack the other XP buffs on top:**
   - Food with the experience line: +5% kill XP for 15 minutes.
   - Cozy Sleeping Bag: +1% XP per minute rested inside it, up to +3%. Use it during natural breaks of about three minutes.
3. **Don't wait it out:** Don't stop playing just to build rested XP. It is a bonus for time you would spend offline anyway.
4. **Rotate alts:** Play one character while the others rest in inns. Play a new alt to about level 5, until the quests send you to the starter inn, then park it there.

## Recent changes that affect where you spend it

Dungeon XP has changed several times since launch, so older guides may be wrong about it:

- **8 November 2026:** Blizzard increased XP from dungeon enemy kills by 50%.
- **13 November 2026:** A hotfix removed XP from dungeon kills whenever a much higher-level player was in the group.
- **14 to 15 November 2026:** Blizzard relaxed that rule. XP is now removed only when the high-level player is more than 12 levels above the enemies.

The practical result: dungeons are now one of the best places to spend rested XP, unless someone in your group is more than 12 levels above the mobs. If so, nobody gets kill XP from those mobs, and rested XP is not used either.

## Where the sources disagree

- The guides disagree on whether lying in the sleeping bag while online also builds rested XP, or only gives the +3% buff. They do agree that logging out in it counts as resting.
- They also disagree on how long the sleeping bag's +3% buff lasts: some say one hour, others two hours.
